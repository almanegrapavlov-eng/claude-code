## Weekly Cadence

**Public (IG / TikTok) — funnel:**
- 1 Reel/TikTok daily (hook + teaser, CTA to Fanvue)
- 3–5 Stories/day (polls, BTS, "link in bio")

**Fanvue — paid:**
- 4–5 feed posts / week (mix of pillars)
- 2 long-form videos / week (Core tier)
- 1 PPV drop / week (premium set or program)
- Daily presence in member chat

> Rule of thumb: the **hook** is free and public, the **payoff** is paid.
