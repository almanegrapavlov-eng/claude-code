rebuilt her relationship with movement around feeling good rather than punishing
herself. She now coaches a small online community on sustainable fitness,
mobility, and recovery, and documents the "strong body / soft life" balance —
hard training *and* slow mornings, smoothies, and rest. Warm, a little goofy,
allergic to gatekeeping.

## 2. Character Bible (consistency anchors — keep these IDENTICAL every time)

These traits are what make every photo recognizably *her*. Always include them
in generation prompts (see `generation-queue.md` for the reusable base prompt).

- **Age:** ~26
- **Build:** athletic, toned, hourglass; ~5'7"
- **Skin:** sun-kissed golden tan, healthy glow
- **Hair:** honey-brown, mid-length, usually a high ponytail or loose beach waves
- **Eyes:** hazel-green
- **Distinctive marks:** light freckles across nose & cheeks; **small beauty mark above the left side of the upper lip** (key consistency anchor)
- **Smile:** warm, natural, slight dimples
- **Styling:** natural "no-makeup" makeup, dainty gold jewelry (thin necklace + small hoops)
- **Wardrobe default:** matching athleisure sets in earth tones & sage green

> Tip: the beauty mark + freckles + hazel-green eyes are the three details that
> most reliably keep an AI face looking like the *same person* shot to shot.

## 3. Visual Identity

**Brand colors**

| Role | Color | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | Sage green | `#9CAF88` |
| Secondary | Warm sand | `#E4D5C7` |
| Accent | Terracotta | `#C77B58` |
| Background | Off-white | `#F7F4EF` |
| Text / contrast | Charcoal | `#2E2E2E` |

**Aesthetic:** sunlit, airy, editorial-but-candid. Natural light, film grain,
shallow depth of field. Think "Pinterest wellness" — never harsh studio flash.

**Typography suggestion:** a clean geometric sans for headings (e.g. Poppins /
Outfit) + a soft serif for accents (e.g. Fraunces).

---

## 4. Voice & Tone

- Warm, upbeat, encouraging — talks like a supportive friend, not a drill sergeant.
- Body-positive and anti-"grind culture." Sustainable > extreme.
- Playful, a little self-deprecating. Emojis: ☀️🌿💪🥤🧘‍♀️ (used sparingly).
- **Never:** shame-based language, crash diets, medical claims, "get abs in 7 days" hype.

**Sample lines**
- "Reminder: rest days are training days too. 🌿"
- "Did the workout, then immediately made a smoothie the size of my head. Balance."
- "You don't have to earn your food, babe. Eat the carbs, lift the weights."

## 6. Compliance & Disclosure (do not skip)

- **Disclose the AI nature.** Fanvue's policies and FTC/advertising guidance
  require AI creators to be clearly labeled. Put **"AI creator / virtual persona"**
  in the bio and profile. This builds trust and keeps the account safe from bans.
- **Fictional only.** Kaia is a wholly fictional character — not based on, and
  not resembling, any real person. Do not train her face on real individuals' photos.
- **SFW scope.** This kit and all generated content stay non-explicit.
- **No medical/financial claims.** Frame fitness/nutrition as general wellness, not advice.
- **Read the platform ToS** for the latest AI-content and verification rules before launch.
